Native/source-page placement
Scope: OSA, GERD/Mental Health, and Lumbar packages; apply in the shared renderer.

Align preserved source pages and source-page images to the reviewer content left
margin and maximize within the available content box. Do not center a narrower
fixed-width source block when more horizontal space is available.

Acceptance criteria:
- No extra left indentation beyond the normal reviewer-page content margin.
- Scale uniformly as large as possible within the usable width and height;
  preserve aspect ratio. Height may limit width for a tall source page.
- Keep portrait pages portrait and landscape pages properly oriented, honoring
  source orientation without double rotation.
- Keep reviewer headers and footers outside the source image area.
- Do not crop or alter the original source page just to fill space.
- Preserve margins contained in the original source in the original-page view.
- When graphs or tables remain difficult to read, add a separate, clearly
  identified enlargement while retaining the complete original-page view.

Distinguish the two whitespace problems:
1. EMF-added whitespace: eliminate added indentation, centering, padding, and
   unnecessarily narrow source blocks by left-aligning and maximizing placement.
2. Original-source whitespace: preserve it in the complete source-page view;
   use a separate figure/table enlargement when readability requires it.
